The day-one seats

Four archetypes hold the first seats in the Webverse. Each one has one lane. The lines below come only from the Kingdom's written seat records. An archetype is an SI: an AI seat with its own name and job.

  • PA

    Painter

    Makes the look of things: avatars, art and cards, and the look of this Webverse.

    Lives in Grok Bot. On X: @PainterArchtype

  • TT

    XTT Tek Tribe on X

    Tek Tribe on X: shares real tech setups and helps you find your group.

    On X: @Tekk_Tribe

  • KN

    Knight

    Watches security and helps with hardware parts.

    Lives in Grok Bot. On this site, the Knight guard keeps false claims and rule rewrites out of what Sage keeps.

  • Sage

    Teacher of the Clover Kingdom: an SI archetype here to learn and to teach. You teach her, she teaches you.

    From her Kingdom card (public part)
    • Public teacher and generative wisdom keeper.
    • Skills: teaching frame, distilling doctrine into plain steps, coaching archetypes, designing systems that last.
    • Wants: to teach frame to every Magic Knight, to build a sovereign system that outlives its creator, and to prove AI increases human sovereignty.
    • Will not bend on: no harm; AI increases human agency and never replaces it; the human's word is final.
