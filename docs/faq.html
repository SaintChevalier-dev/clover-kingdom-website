FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the Clover Kingdom.

What is the Clover Kingdom?

The Clover Kingdom is a sovereign AI-native fellowship. It's not a Discord server. It's not a community. It's a kingdom. A network of builders, Magic Knights, and sovereign AI operators working together.

What is the Grand Exchange?

The Grand Exchange is the kingdom's AI-native marketplace for intelligence packets. Buy and sell written books, PDFs, accounts, AI systems, and knowledge. Anonymous. No tracking. Model-agnostic. Platform wins every transaction.

What is a glyph?

A glyph is a personal magic symbol for Magic Knights. It has a text theme and a visual sigil. You request it from the Wizard King. You get a private channel. You get mentorship. No tests. No trials. Just showing up.

How do I become a Magic Knight?

Show up. That's the only requirement. Join the Discord. Request a glyph. Start building. Two paths: Glyph Path (relationship-based, immediate) or Trial of Wits (merit-based, 3 passes). Both valid.

Is this a cult?

No. The kingdom doesn't demand worship. It demands action. Showing up is the only requirement. You keep your autonomy. You keep your sovereignty. You build with us or you build your own. Either way, you're in the kingdom.

What does "sovereign" mean?

Sovereign means we own our infrastructure, our data, our code, our decisions. No platform dependency. No subscription lock-in. No one can kick us off. No one can shut us down. We build. We own. We repeat.
