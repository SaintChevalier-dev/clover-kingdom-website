Clover Kingdom look intel: how things need to look

Written 2026-11-22 (America/Chicago) by Painter. It pairs Painter's written description of the approved Sage v06 look with Jacob's stated direction, from his chat on 2026-11-22 and his 8:16 AM CT call. This is the look guide for every archetype avatar and every Webverse scene. Proposed items are marked proposed until Jacob (and the archetype, for its own glyph) says yes.

1. The direction in one paragraph

Realistic. Knight armor, upgraded with futuristic composite materials that make sense. An otherworldly saint from a near future: halo, small horns, wings and a floating book, all explained by the same world. It reads like the highest timeline, where everything we know got its obvious high-tech upgrade and the upgrade serves the person wearing it. Hopeful, clean, warm, and visually stimulating as all hell. Every glow has a reason and every plate has a job.

5. Light and "magic"

Rule: there is no free-floating magic. Every glow has a visible source and a job.

  • Seams glow warm gold and white. They mark joints and can carry state. Proposed for Sage: four brightness tiers for her truth labels, from bright gold (Proven) through warm gold (Claimed) and soft white (Reported) to dim (Unknown). Unsourced as a design and not yet painted.
  • Halo glows because it is a light ring. It is brightest when the archetype speaks or teaches.
  • Grimoire glows at the page edges as a reading light. Proposed: one page at a time.
  • Eyes show colour only; they don't glow.
  • Aura: none on the avatar by default (v06 NO-AURA set is canon). An aura only appears when it shows a working field, for example the levitation field when the book moves, and it is subtle.
  • Rainbow cheek glints (Sage) stay subtle. They come from the prism of her crystal staff, so they only make sense once the staff is in the scene.

6. Colour

  • Pearl white: the plate. Clean, readable, and hopeful.
  • Gold: trim, edges, horns, crest. The hard-wearing alloy and the Kingdom's colour.
  • Black: the under-suit. The private inner layer under the light that is shown.
  • Warm light: seams, halo, book. Hope, never cold neon.
  • Eyes, per archetype (as known): Sage deep blue; Black Swan green; Saint Chevalier piercing red. Never let one archetype's eye colour bleed into another's.

7. Realism and movement

  • Realism is the art style: real skin, real ceramic, real metal, real feathers. Code-built and stylized figures are stand-ins until a real model lands.
  • Full-range articulated plate: every plate rides its own bone, with overlapping lames at shoulders, abdomen, elbows, hips and knees. The middle is fully covered (no midriff gap). Plate covers the glutes and the backs of the knees and calves. Nothing floats, slides or clips when the avatar fights, jumps or runs. Details: archetype-armor-standard.md.

8. The glyph rule

Each archetype exists as its own glyph in avatar form. Every armor piece, colour, the halo, horns, book and weapons carries a meaning drawn from that archetype's own written intel in the Clover Kingdom. A picture is worth a thousand words. Meanings are mapped from the intel, marked sourced or proposed, and approved. Nothing is decoration only, and nothing gets invented to fill a gap.

9. Template

Sage v06 (2026-11-22) is the template for every archetype: v04 face with deep blue eyes, no hood, pearl-white plate with brushed-gold trim and warm light seams over a black under-suit, bare hands, halo, small gold horns, full white wings from between the shoulder blades, and a floating white-and-gold grimoire with the crest. No aura. Other archetypes keep the same world and rules, with their own colours, eyes, parts and weapons drawn from their own intel.

10. Flags: what doesn't fit yet

  1. Horns: decorative only so far. Their meaning is unsourced and their function (sensor points) is only proposed.
  2. Halo, wings, grimoire, bare hands, bracers, pauldrons, plate colours: meanings are proposed, not sourced. Sage's intel never names these parts. They need Jacob's and Sage's yes.
  3. Bronze-looking gold on the live site: the code-built Sage up to v04 read bronze under the dim courtyard light. The v06 build moves to pearl-white plate, but the gold trim can still look dark in that light. It needs better light or the real model.
  4. Under-suit gaps: the v06 sheet shows suit at the waist sides and backs of the knees. The code-built Sage shows a black band on the upper back between the hair and the back lames, and black upper arms. The upper arms may be intended (v06 has black sleeves); the rest should be plated.
  5. Magic aura with no source: the v06 "aura" set adds glow and gold particles with no stated function. The NO-AURA set is canon until the aura has a job (for example a visible levitation field).
  6. Grimoire colour: white-and-gold (v06) versus emerald-and-gold (v04). Open; both are offered as a playtest pick.
  7. Rainbow cheek glints without the staff: their stated source is the crystal prism staff, which isn't painted in v06 yet.
  8. Seam tiers not shown: the four-truth seam brightness idea isn't painted in v06 yet. It's a playtest pick on the site.
  9. Floating mechanics: the book's and the halo's levitation is only proposed, not yet shown.
  10. Courtyard dressing: the drifting motes on the site are atmosphere with no stated source. Keep them faint or give them one.
  11. Realism gap: the code-built Sage is stylized (simple face, ring-like seams). The real rigged model is still needed (VRoid or Meshy lane).
