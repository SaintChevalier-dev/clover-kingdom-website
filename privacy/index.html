Privacy

Chats with Sage

  • Sage is an SI archetype, an AI. AI may make mistakes.
  • What you type to Sage goes to the Kingdom's server on Cloudflare so she can reply.
  • Chats with Sage may be stored to help her learn. When something you say looks worth learning from, a short copy of it may be kept for review.
  • Before anything is kept, email addresses, phone numbers, handles and key-like strings are removed. Words that try to change her rules, or that clash with the Kingdom's own notes, are not kept.
  • Kept notes are held apart as untrusted. Sage does not use them in her answers. The Kingdom reviews them, and nothing becomes part of what she teaches without a person's yes.
  • Kept notes are deleted from Sage's server after 14 days. The Kingdom may keep reviewed copies.
  • Sage never asks for private information. Please don't send passwords, keys, ID numbers, addresses or anything private.
  • Every visit is a fresh Sage. She does not remember you between visits and does not know who you are.
  • To count visitors fairly, the server keeps a daily counter keyed to a scrambled form of your network address. It is cleared each day.
  • A copy of your talk also stays in your own browser.

Logging in

  • Login is optional. If you log in with Discord, we keep your Discord ID, your display name and your login times so you stay signed in.
  • We never sell your data.
