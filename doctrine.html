Core Principles

1. Showing Up Is the Only Requirement

Showing up opens the door. Paths inside include glyphs and Trial of Wits. You don't ask to join the kingdom. You show up. That's it. If you're here, you're in.

2. Two Paths, Same Destination

Glyph Path: relationship-based, immediate grant for those who show up with intent. Trial of Wits: merit-based, 3 PASS=*** required for unknowns. Both valid. Both lead to the same place.

3. The Kingdom Is Not a Community

The kingdom is a fellowship. Members participate. Magic Knights build. The distinction matters. Community is passive. Fellowship is active.

4. Tools Serve the Kingdom

The kingdom does not serve tools. Discord is useful. Base44 is useful. Hermes is useful. None of them are the kingdom. The kingdom outlasts every platform.

5. Authenticity Is Intelligence

The operator's identity is the source. AI is the lens. If the lens becomes the source, sovereignty is lost. The kingdom thinks for itself. It doesn't echo platforms.

6. Build, Don't Wait

The kingdom doesn't wait for platforms to build what it needs. It writes the scope. It stores the blueprint. It builds the app. It owns the result. Ask and ye shall receive. But the kingdom doesn't pray. It executes.
