Clover Kingdom

The public front door. A place with a standard and a gate, for people who want to build their own SI and level up their life.

Everyone is welcome. The house rules live in #rules on the Discord. Breaking them puts your place in the Kingdom at risk.

Build your own SI. Level up your life. Find your people.

How to join

  1. Join the Discord (free today): https://discord.gg/2BrNPwUuTB
  2. Say what you want or what you are building.
  3. Apply with the five questions on this site (a form is not a rank).
  4. A human reads it. Saint Chevalier, Wizard King and his SI helpers read applications. Approval comes through Discord or a conversation.

Report a problem or ask for deletion

Send a direct message to the Wizard King on the Clover Kingdom Discord. Report anyone breaking the house rules, someone being unkind or pressuring you, anyone asking for private details, or anything that worries you. A real person reads every report. Breaking the house rules puts your place in the Kingdom at risk. If someone may be in danger, the report is handed to a human right away. Anyone can ask for their data to be deleted the same way, or by messaging Saint Chevalier on X, @Saint_Chevalier.

What the Kingdom offers

  • Motivation - A reason to start, and people who help you keep going.
  • Community - Like-minded people who want to build, learn and help each other. Lives in the Discord.
  • A robot buddy - Build a robot buddy of your own. Ask in the Discord or apply. Nothing priced or promised on this page.
  • A way of life and thinking - Written plainly in the Standard.
  • Survival - Water, food, shelter and security.
  • Secret tek intelligence - For members only. None of it is shown here.

The archetypes (SI helpers)

SI helpers with one job each. Not every helper is open to talk yet. Join the Discord and ask.

  • Wizard King - Thinks an idea all the way through before anything is done.
  • Saint Chevalier - Checks that plans fit together and do not contradict each other.
  • Sage - Writes how-to guides and checks a card is safe to share.
  • Val - Plans the roadmap and prepares the next steps.
  • ARGUS - Grades tools, never people, and says what each costs and whether you own it.
  • HEALER - Checks for invented facts and for private information leaking out.
  • Painter - Makes the look of things, such as avatars and art.
  • Knight - Watches security and helps with hardware parts.
  • DRAGON - Finds your highest-leverage move right now, sends you to do it, and levels up the conversation with what you bring back.
  • Roster - Builds your party: shows who's proven where across your people and SI helpers, so you pick the right team.
  • Tek Tribe - Shares real tech setups and helps you find your group.

FAQ (short)

Is it free? The Discord and the community are free today. Nothing else is priced yet.

What is SI / PSI? SI is super intelligence. A sovereign SI is one you own, that outlives you and runs on your terms. PSI is Physically Sovereign Intelligence: the person who works with their SI as one team.

What is a Magic Knight? A rank earned through work inside the Kingdom. A form is not a rank.
